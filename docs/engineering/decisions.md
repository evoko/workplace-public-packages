# Decisions

Every decision still in force that shapes the code, with its reason. Read this before changing
something that looks arbitrary: it probably is not.

- **Owner** marks a decision the repository owner made or confirmed, with its date. Do not reverse
  one without asking.
- **Taken** marks a choice made while building that the owner has not been asked to confirm. It
  stands until someone has a reason to reopen it; say so in review rather than changing it
  silently.
- **Design** marks a decision of the SOLAR design team, relayed by the owner.

A decision about one component is also written as the `reason` of its rule in
`spec/overlay/<name>.yaml`; that file is the authority for it, and this page only lists the
decisions a reader could not find there. When a decision is made or changed, update this page in
the same change.

## Components, by family

Decisions a reader might not expect. The overlays hold the rest.

| Family            | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Status                       |
