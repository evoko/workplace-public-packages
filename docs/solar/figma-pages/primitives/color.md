## Colors / Brand

Brand colors represent the core visual identity of Biamp within the SOLAR Design System.\
They are used to reinforce brand recognition across products, marketing surfaces, and key interface elements.

Brand tokens provide a consistent way to apply Biamp’s primary brand palette while ensuring predictable behavior across light and dark themes.

Brand colors should be used intentionally and sparingly, typically for primary brand moments, highlights, or branded surfaces rather than general UI structure.
